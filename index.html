About

Analysis from the eastern flank

Fulcrum Brief covers the wars and conflicts shaping the world, wherever they flare up, and what they mean for your money and your life. Every claim is checked and every source is named on screen and in the description.

Each video finds the pivot point of a story, the moment the balance shifted, and ends on what it means for everyday life: prices at the pump, groceries, markets, security.

New full videos every Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

Damyan Ivanov Host

Fulcrum Brief is presented by Damyan Ivanov, based in Eastern Europe on NATO's eastern flank, close to the war in Ukraine and to the shipping lanes and energy routes it keeps hitting.

Each briefing follows the same discipline: find the pivot point, show every source on screen, label claims as claims, and end on what it means for the people watching.
