About

Fulcrum Brief covers Ukraine and Russia, Iran and the Middle East, Israel, the Red Sea, China and Taiwan, and oil and energy. Every claim is checked and every source is named on screen and in the description.

Each video finds the pivot point of a story, the moment the balance shifted, and ends on what it means for everyday life: prices at the pump, groceries, markets, security.
